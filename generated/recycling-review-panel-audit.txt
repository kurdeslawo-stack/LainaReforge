Verified from the committed implementation:

- APPROVED requires recyclable=true and positive integer shards;

- overlapping imports require explicit confirmation before replacing local decisions;
- reset of local decisions requires explicit confirmation;
- export contains only locally completed decisions;
- filters cover status, priority, proposal and acquisition tag;
- search covers name, wiki, material, CMD and model_path;
- navigation includes previous, next, next PENDING, next HIGH and direct item jump;
- fixed shard buttons, custom positive shards, reject and skip actions are present;
- item Wiki links use encoded titles and open with noopener/noreferrer;
- rendered user/item data is inserted with textContent rather than innerHTML;
- embedded JSON escapes control characters and closing HTML tags defensively;
- generator validates the ETAP 4 queue before emitting the panel;
